Scale

Wheelbase is the most accurate. Tire size can be used if you don't have an easy way of measuring; keep in mind tires can vary 2-3% from their stated size. An accurate saddle height measurement is good to know regardless. Entering all three improves the accuracy of the model.

Fit

Drag on the photo: the seat tube angle, the saddle top, the saddle nose and rear, and the stem clamp. The sweet spot is the middle of the saddle, on its top surface; drop and butt to bars are taken from it, as in the ARTEFACT geometry engine. Pulsing yellow points need you; they turn green once placed. A placed point goes yellow again if a later change shifts it more than 0.5% from where you set it.

Frame geometry

Drag the head tube top and bottom onto the tube's faces: the steering axis runs through both. You can also drag the axis line to move it or its arc to turn it, which carries the head tube points along and turns them yellow to recheck. A padlock beside each control locks the angle or the left/right position on its own; dragging a head tube point then keeps whatever is locked. The seat tube angle and seat tube top can be set here too. Pulsing yellow points need you; green ones are placed.
